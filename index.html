Privacy

The short version for each game. Every full policy, including our other apps, is at bilalsafdar4130.github.io/privacy-policies.

Our games have no accounts and send us nothing. When you buy something in a game, Google Play takes the payment under its own terms; we never see your card or billing details. Questions or a data request: bilalsafdar3014@gmail.com.
Gate & GoNo data collected
What it keeps
Progress and settings, stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
Ads and analytics
None. No advertising SDK, no analytics, no advertising ID, no account.
Purchases
Optional, one-time, through Google Play Billing (Company Cash, Investor Deal, Every Livery). Google Play processes the payment; the game never sees your payment details. Everything for sale can also be earned by playing.
Delete your data
In the game: Privacy & data → Delete my data (tap twice). Purchases stay with your Google account; Restore purchases brings them back.
